## Customer

- meat processors

## Offer

- herd health monitoring
- feed scheduling
- milking support
- reporting

## Revenue

- monthly herd-operations platform fee
- per-harvest fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no veterinary medication administration without governor gate
- operations near large livestock always require human sign-off
- herd and health records are auditable, not editable
